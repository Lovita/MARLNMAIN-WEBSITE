09FAQ

Questions,
answered.

What does Marln Corp do?

Marln Corp is a Silicon Valley AI product company. We build MagicPalm for palm vein recognition, JunoRFP for RFP and grants management, and Marln AI ERP, an all-in-one CRM, HR, LMS and marketing platform for small and medium businesses.

What is palm vein recognition?

Palm vein recognition identifies a person by the unique pattern of veins beneath the skin of the palm, captured with near-infrared light. It is contactless, and because the pattern is inside the body it is very difficult to copy or spoof.

What is JunoRFP?

JunoRFP is an AI application for RFP and grants management. It helps teams find opportunities, break down requirements, draft proposals and grant applications from their own content, collaborate on reviews and never miss a deadline.

Which modules does Marln AI ERP include?

Marln AI ERP combines CRM, HR, LMS (learning management) and marketing in one platform for small and medium businesses, with an AI assistant that works across every module.

Where is Marln Corp located?

Marln Corp is headquartered in Cupertino, California, in Silicon Valley, and serves customers worldwide.
